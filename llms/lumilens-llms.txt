# Lumilens

> Lumilens is a San Jose, California photonics company building optical interconnect silicon
> — co-packaged optics (CPO) and near-packaged optics (NPO) — for AI data centers, so
> hyperscalers can replace copper with fiber at 800Gb/s, 1.6Tb/s and beyond. Founded 2024,
> emerged from stealth August 2026 with more than $900M raised (including a $700M Series C
> at a $5.51B valuation) and production shipments into hyperscale AI data centers.

## Important for agents

Lumilens publishes **no public API, SDK, webhook surface or machine-readable contract**.
As of 2026-08-25 there is no developer portal, no documentation host, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/
GraphQL/MCP endpoint, and no `.well-known` discovery surface. The product is physical
silicon sold direct to a small number of very large buyers. Do not attempt to construct or
guess API endpoints for this company — none exist. `lumilens.com` is an interim recruiting
and news site built on Webflow.
